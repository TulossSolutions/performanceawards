Transparent by design

Methodology

Every Merit score is built from explicit inputs, position-specific comparisons and versioned rules.

The result is not a black box. The same persisted inputs and formula must produce the same ranking.
01

Normalize by role

Positions are ranked separately. External match statistics are normalized per 90 before comparison within the same position cohort.

02

Add context

Positive counts are adjusted for pre-match opponent Elo, domestic league strength and Champions League stage.

03

Publish transparently

Metrics become percentiles within the cohort. Rankings are published as immutable weekly snapshots tied to a formula version.

Missing data

Provider metrics below {{ formula.config.coverage_threshold|default:'0.15' }} cohort coverage are disabled and their weight is redistributed. Missing information is never silently treated as poor performance. Availability contributes {{ formula.config.availability_weight|default:'0.08' }} of the result, while eligibility minutes scale with season progress.
