Methodology

The ranking is deterministic, role-specific, context-aware and versioned. The result should always be explainable from the underlying data.

Positions are ranked separately. External match statistics are normalized per 90, and positive counts are adjusted for pre-match opponent Elo, domestic league strength, and Champions League stage. Metrics become percentiles within the same position cohort.

Missing provider metrics are disabled cohort-wide below {{ formula.config.coverage_threshold|default:'0.15' }} coverage and their weight is redistributed; absence is never silently treated as zero. Availability contributes {{ formula.config.availability_weight|default:'0.08' }} of the result, while eligibility minutes scale with season progress. Rankings are versioned weekly snapshots.
